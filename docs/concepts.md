# Tomodachi: working concepts

What the demo proves today, and the rules we've settled on. Plans and open questions are
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues); the research behind them is
mapped in [research/README.md](research/README.md).

## The pitch

See the root level README.md

**The core: Tomo comes to you.** You never have to find the discipline to open a learning app every day. Tomo drops in through the day for a 10–30 second moment, then leaves. What it brings comes from a **content source**. Today the only source is the age track (the README's idea). Other sources, like an Anki deck, are ideas ([#11](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/11), [#12](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/12)).

## What works

| Concept | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Lives in the notch** | Tomo sits small beside the notch, opens into a card, and its eyes follow your cursor. Built on the [Coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou) notch app (MIT code). |
| **First run** | On the Mac, a new learner's first launch opens a short welcome window before Tomo's first visit, following the reference app in [#83](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/83): an egg in the colours of the learner's Tomo rocks until tapped, cracks, and Tomo pops out (ばあ！); Tomo's first word, asked with a guide ("Try it now") and counted like any answer; "You just understood your first Japanese", with when the word comes back and why; how Tomo comes to you by the notch; how often (the visit choices); and what 1さい to 3さい bring. Then Tomo pops out of the notch. Closing the window counts as done. Learners who already have a Tomo don't see it; a Mac that joins the learner's iCloud Tomo during it gets "welcome back" (おかえり！). On the iPhone it's full screen, with the same hatch and first word, then the reference's own steps: how Tomo comes to you there (a notification, the Lock Screen), the rhythm, quiet hours, "Let words find you" (the only place the notification prompt appears), how to add a widget, and Tomo's Lock Screen card, yes or not now. A phone that joins the iCloud Tomo gets "welcome back", then those steps. |
| **Own character** | Every Tomo is a blob of its own, drawn and animated by our own code. A seed made when it first appears picks what it keeps for life: its colour, its eyes (bars, dots, big shiny ones, sleepy lids, tiny beads; rarely just one eye), a resting mouth, markings in a second colour (a belly, a cap, spots, stripes, two tones) and, from 2さい, something on its head (cat, bunny or bear ears, horns, an antenna, a sprout). So no two learners' Tomos look alike, and the Mac and iPhone show the same one. Each birthday it evolves: it squeezes down, flashes and pops out in a new shape and proportions, a bit bigger; at 2, 4 and 6さい it also gains an extra (rosy cheeks, freckles, a shine, a little mark). Rare shapes are lucky finds. Widgets, the Lock Screen and the app icon show the yellow Tomodachi mascot. Starting over hatches a new Tomo. It never sits still: it breathes, blinks, follows your cursor, fidgets between events, and dozes off if ignored. Nothing of Coucou's Mochi character is left. |
| **Levels and ages** | Tomo has levels: small sets of words. When 9 in 10 of a level's words reach "knows it", Tomo levels up (Lv 2, Lv 3…), with a sparkle and a chirp, and the next set unlocks. Some levels are birthdays, like a Pokémon evolving: 1さい speaks single baby words (ワンワン, まんま), 2さい evolves and uses two-word phrases (ワンワン いた！), 3さい has real short conversations. The header shows "1さい · Lv 2". A full-width experience bar under it moves a little with every answer that counts (each stage one of the level's best 9 words reaches, up to "knows it"). Its last tenth is the goal, set apart: it fills only when the level is done, so the bar never looks full before. It never moves back: a slip sets the word back, not the bar, and that word's part turns pale until it's answered right again. Under Tomo's word the card says what's left: "1 more word to Lv 2 · ready now", or "· next at 8:37". Level and age never go down. Japanese goes to 6さい (274 levels), English to 3さい (66 levels); Spanish has small placeholders. |
| **Word stages** | Every word has a stage, following WaniKani's published rules in our own code: just heard (1–4) → knows it (5–6) → good at it (7) → loves it (8) → forever (9, never asked again). A right answer moves a word up one stage when it's due, or early once at least half its wait has passed; Tomo asks again after 4 h, 8 h, 1 day, 2 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, then 4 months (levels 1–2 start faster). Wrong tries in the same round move it down, twice as far from "knows it" up. Right after the hint, it stays. Answering again sooner is practice: Tomo still reacts, nothing moves (the badge says **Practice**) |
| **Seeing Tomo grow** | Settings → **Tomo**: a live Tomo, its age and level, the bar to the next level and what's left ("1 more word to Lv 2 · ready now"), days together, each age with its levels and what it brings (grown / now / later), and today's new words, answers and words that got stronger. Settings → **Words**: every level, locked ones too, with each word's picture, reading, meaning, stage and when it's due. Click Tomo's age in the notch header for the Tomo page; menu bar → Tomo's words… for the Words page. |
| **Saved progress** | Tomo's level, age and every word's stage are saved on the device, one Tomo per language pair, and synced through the learner's iCloud: the Mac and the iPhone share one Tomo. When both already had one, they merge (the higher level, each word's latest answer); a start over reaches the other device too. Every answer is logged, to tune the rules later. Start over (menu bar, Settings → Tomo) asks first; it's never one click away in the notch. |
| **Understanding before speaking** | 1–2さい: each word is asked the best way it can be: **pick the picture** (about 290 words have one), **do what Tomo asks** (feed / bed / hug), or **pick the meaning** out of three, which works for every word. 3さい: Tomo asks real questions (なに してるの？), and you **pick what it means** (a new question) or **pick a reply that fits** (later reviews), out of three. Typed and spoken answers are switched off for now; when they're back, romaji is read as kana. |
| **Baby talk + grown-up word** | Tomo says ワンワン, and after you get it the card shows "grown-ups say: いぬ（犬）". |
| **Drop-in visits** | Tomo opens on its own every so often (20 min by default; 10 min to 2 h, or only when clicked, in Settings), starting at launch, for up to 3 answers: words that are due first, then at most one new word (10 new a day by default; 5–30 in Settings). A scheduled visit with nothing due or new is skipped. Ignore it for 10 s (20 s at 3さい), or close it with × (or Esc once you've clicked it; a visit never takes your keyboard), and it tucks back in with a red dot and an occasional bounce until you check in. Visits wait while you're typing and skip if you're away (no permission needed). Tomo follows the notch when displays change. |
| **Always there** | Between visits, click small Tomo any time for free play with no time limit. Playing by choice counts: it brings due words, then new ones (within the daily limit), then words far enough along to review early. When none of those are left, Tomo rests: happy and moving as always, it says "また あとで あそぼ！", and the card says when it's back ("Back at 10:12") and what's left before the next level. Resting holds: when it tucks back in, when you click small Tomo again and when you come back to the iPhone app, it's still resting, never asking again; when something counts while the card is open, Tomo notices and asks it. Practice is a quieter button on the resting card, like WaniKani's Extra Study, and says it won't move the bar. While practicing, the header says "Practice until 6:31 AM" and the bar dims; practice lasts until something counts or Tomo is opened again, and rotates through the words closest to due. |
| **Child-like feedback** | Right: hop, happy wiggle, sparkles, praise. Wrong: shake, > < eyes and ちがう〜. Not understood: head tilt, a "?" and ん？ わかんない… Poked: a squish and いたい！ (three pokes make it dizzy) |
| **Help when you're stuck** | A prototype. Help opens in a large-text panel that grows out of the notch under Tomo's card. Click a word for its card (meaning in your language, the Mac dictionary, Open in Zenbu). Drag across words → "I don't understand" → an explanation of just that part. **Hint** shows the meaning and example answers (click one to use it). **Explain** gives the key parts and a tip, and can ask Tomo to say it simpler. Tomo doesn't leave while help is open. |
| **Every answer has an obvious result** | **✓ Win +1** (green): understood or right picture; the word moves up a stage. **✓ Practice** (blue, no green wash): right, but nothing counts right now, so the bar doesn't move. **✗ Miss** (red): tried, Tomo didn't get it, or wrong picture. **– No score** (grey): wrong language, or a help request ("what?", なに？); no credit, no penalty. Help requests repeat the question slowly and open the hint. |
| **Sound effects** | Tomo peeps and chirps for each moment: a visit starting, Win, Miss, No score, growing up, being poked. The island blips when it opens and closes. All synthesized in code; the peeps get lower as Tomo grows. One switch with the voice. |
| **Voice** | Tomo speaks with macOS's voice for the language at a raised pitch, free and offline. Spoken answers (Apple's on-device recognition) are switched off while talking questions are choices, so the app doesn't ask for the microphone. |
| **AI is optional** | Every age is scripted for now (3さい questions are multiple choice). AI is used for word cards and explanations, and will judge typed answers when they come back; without it, a placeholder keyword matcher stands in until the Zenbu offline dictionary system replaces it. |
| **Settings** | Laid out like the Mac's System Settings: a sidebar (Tomo, Words, General, AI, About) and the selected page. General: languages, how often Tomo visits (or only when clicked), new words a day, voice, open at login (off until you turn it on). Testing (try another age, skip ahead a day, both on a copy of Tomo in memory) shows only after ⌥-clicking the menu bar icon. Start over is on the Tomo page. |
| **Any language pair** | What you speak and what you learn are separate settings (Settings → General). Packs: Japanese, English, Spanish; interface in English or Japanese, so a Japanese speaker can learn English. Details: [languages.md](languages.md). |
| **Reminders (iPhone)** | Tomo comes to you on the iPhone with a notification when words are ready: a new word, or one that's due. The learner picks the rhythm in the first run (15 min, 30 min, hourly, the default, 2 h, 4 h or daily) and quiet hours (9 PM to 8 AM by default). Rules: only when something counts, never sooner than one rhythm after playing, never in quiet hours (a word due at night waits for the morning; daily is each morning), at the rhythm for two days if ignored, then once a morning, and nothing a week after the first, until the learner is back. Each is Tomo speaking at its age, with a translation and what's waiting ("あそぼ！" / "Let's play! · 3 words waiting"). Tapping one opens the app, which is free play, so Tomo asks right away. Settings for them are a view ready for the iPhone's Settings ([#90](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/90)). |
| **On the iPhone** | On TestFlight. The same Tomo on one screen, big and alive, with the same rounds, results and growth as the notch card; opening the app is free play. What's left to grow is under the bar, and when nothing counts Tomo rests on the screen and says when it's back, with Practice as a button. Tap a word in Tomo's line for its word card (the line's meaning from the pack, the AI's meaning of the word if a key is set, Open in Zenbu, and the iPhone's built-in dictionary). Home Screen widgets (small, medium) show Tomo, its age and level, and a red dot with "3 words waiting" when something counts; Lock Screen widgets show the age, level, bar and what's waiting. On the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (once the learner says yes, in the first run), a Live Activity shows Tomo beside two rows: what's waiting ("A new word is ready to learn"), then age, level and the experience bar, with a round Play button; when nothing counts, Tomo sleeps and the card says when the next words come ("New words at 10:12"), turning to "ready" by itself. Play opens one round right on the card: Tomo's word and its choices; a miss marks the choice and Tomo shakes it off, a right answer counts like in the app and shows the reading and meaning before the card invites again. Tapping anywhere else opens the app; the compact Dynamic Island says "あそぼ！". On all of these Tomo moves, a new pose every second, and holds still once iOS stops ticking seconds on the Lock Screen. No mic, Explain or Settings yet ([#52](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/52)). |
| **Any AI provider** | One adapter: Anthropic's own API, plus anything that speaks the OpenAI chat format (OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama, custom). Pick it under Settings → **AI** (key in the Keychain), or put `OPENAI_API_KEY` in `.env` and launch with `mac-demo/run.sh`. Tested with OpenAI `gpt-5.4-mini` (about 2 s a reply) and a local Ollama. |

## Rules we've settled on

- **Tomo comes to you.** The visits are the product; the material is a content source that can be swapped.
- **No study sessions.** Learning happens in short visits. Free play is always available but never required.
- **No guilt.** No streak shaming. Tomo is happy to see you, never disappointed in you.
- **Grade whether you were understood, not your pronunciation.** If Tomo doesn't get it, it reacts like a child (ん？), not like a teacher.
- **Grows over days, not minutes.** A word only moves up when it's due (or at least halfway there), so knowing it takes several visits over days. You can't cram; practice in between is welcome but doesn't count.
- **Check offline first, call AI only for leftovers** (planned). Most toddler questions have expected answers that can be checked without AI (yes/no, pick an animal or a food). AI handles the long tail.

## How Tomo grows

The rules are in `TomoProgress.swift`; the levels come from the language pack, built from Wordbank's toddler word data (CDI): each word's age is when half of children understand it (by 19 months: 1さい) or say it (24–35 months: 2さい; later: 3さい), earliest first, in levels of 10. **Japanese, up to 6さい:** 2,712 words in 274 levels. Lv 1–15 are 1さい (バイバイ, ママ, ワンワン, まんま…), Lv 16–60 2さい, Lv 61 is 3さい (talking starts), then Lv 92 4さい, Lv 121 5さい, Lv 180–274 6さい. Past 3, the words come from NINJAL's preschool and picture-book word lists with English meanings from JMdict (ages assigned by how many preschoolers used a word and in how many books it appears; an assumption to tune). **English** (for Japanese speakers; each word has a Japanese meaning): 647 words; Lv 1–26 are 1さい (Daddy, Hi, Mommy, Ball, Dog…), Lv 27–56 2さい, Lv 57 talking, Lv 58–66 later words. Built by `mac-demo/scripts/build-levels.py` ([languages.md](languages.md)). At the fastest, a level takes a few days, so 2さい is weeks away and 3さい months; pacing is [#38](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/38). Spanish still has small placeholder levels.

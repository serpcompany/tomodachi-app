Tomo Decision Lab

Every open question from #137, the iPhone's count and the friends idea, each with a working mockup. Click an option to see it, then comment on anything. Claude reads the comments back.

How to review

  1. Click an option to see it in the mockup.
  2. Press Comment on a decision, or use comment mode and click anywhere on the page.
  3. Say which option you mean. A comment can be a pick, a question or an idea.

Tomo is drawn by a JavaScript port of TomoLook.swift and TomoCharacter.swift (main at f8b9cd0), so shapes, colours and faces follow the real rules. The pouty and lonely faces, the hands and the fluffy texture are sketches for these decisions only.

Tomo's words come from the Japanese pack. Numbers in the stats, friends and settings mockups are examples, not anyone's progress.

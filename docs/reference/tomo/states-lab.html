    Prototype for #251. Tomo here is the JavaScript port of TomoLook.swift and TomoCharacter.swift used by the other design pages, in its jelly material, with the states added on top. The real Tomo stays in Swift.

    Everything is drawn in code each frame: no images, no sprites. Moss, the sweat drop, the rain and the cloud are fixed fills worked out from the clock, so nothing emits at rest; only moments (a hatch, a brush-off, a birthday) give off sparkles, spores or confetti.

    The egg is a port of the first-run egg (TomoEggView), with its rocking, its crack and its seeded spots. Your seed, age and picks are kept in this browser only.

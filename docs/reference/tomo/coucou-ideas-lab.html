Coucou Ideas Lab

Eight ideas from Coucou’s Mac and iPhone screens, made for a friend who teaches Japanese instead of an agent monitor. Ranked best first. Each one switches between what Tomo does today and the proposal, at real size, and you can click through it.

How to review

  1. Flip Today and Proposed on an idea, and click the mockup: tiles, chips, buttons and Tomo itself work.
  2. Press Comment to say yes, no, or what to change. That’s how you pick.
  3. Notch mockups are 1 pt to 1 px on a 760 pt slice of desktop; phones are 393 × 852 pt.

Tomo is drawn by a JavaScript port of TomoLook.swift and TomoCharacter.swift, the same one the Tomo Decision Lab uses. Nothing here is traced from Coucou: the badges, tints and layouts are our own drawings of techniques seen in Coucou’s screens. Mochi, its outfits, expressions, hands and animations stay reserved (#109).

Tomo’s words come from the Japanese pack (levels 1–12, the invite, the rest and reminder lines). Lines marked “new pack line” would need adding. Numbers in Tomo’s week are examples, not anyone’s progress.

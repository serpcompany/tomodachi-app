# Decisions: October 7, 2026

The fifth day of the demo, newest first. Later decisions are in [decisions.md](decisions.md); what Tomo teaches is in [decisions-content.md](decisions-content.md).

### 2026-10-07: On the iPhone, Tomo comes to you with reminders on the reference's cadence; the Lock Screen card is opt-in
The iPhone's first run follows #83 further than the Mac's: after the first word come the rhythm (15 min to daily, **hourly by default**), **quiet hours, 9 PM to 8 AM by default**, "Let words find you" (the only place the iOS notification prompt appears, after its reason), the widget how-to, and the Lock Screen card, which starts only if the learner says yes. Reminders are local notifications planned from progress (`TomoReminders.plan`): only when something counts, never sooner than one rhythm after playing, never in quiet hours (daily is each morning), in Tomo's words at its age. Ignored, they stay at the rhythm for two days, then come once a morning, and stop a week after the first. The settings stay on each device; a learner who had the card before keeps it. **Why:** App Review rejected 1.0 partly because nothing said how Tomo comes back, and the owner asked to copy the reference's cadence and defaults. A reminder with a word behind it every time keeps "no guilt" honest, and the back-off spares a learner who stepped away from fifty pings. The card used to start at launch without asking.

### 2026-10-07: Tomodachi is a regular Mac app, with a Dock icon and a main menu
The owner's call. Tomodachi always has a Dock icon and a main menu whose shortcuts work while it's active: Tomodachi (About, Settings… ⌘,, Quit), Tomo (Drop in now ⌘D, Tomo's words ⌘W, Start Tomo over…, which asks first) and, with the testing tools on, Testing (⌘3, ⌘G, ⌘L, ⌘B, Back to my Tomo). Clicking the Dock icon, or opening the app again from Finder, opens the Tomodachi window. The menu bar icon stays, with the same commands (`TomoMenus`). The island doesn't change: it's a non-activating panel, so Tomo's visits never activate the app or take focus, and hiding the app leaves it in the notch. Launching at login shows only Tomo in the notch. `Info.plist` still starts the app as an agent (`LSUIElement`), and `AppDelegate` makes it regular at launch, so a test run (`TOMO_HEADLESS`) never shows a Dock icon, not even for a moment. **Why:** as a menu-bar accessory Tomodachi was never the active app, so its menu shortcuts never fired, and the Mac app didn't feel like the iPhone's.

### 2026-10-07: One set of screens on the Mac and the iPhone: a window with a sidebar, and tabs
Tomo, Words, Settings and About are SwiftUI views in TomoCore (`TomoGrowthScreen`, `TomoWordsScreen`, `TomoSettingsScreen`, `TomoAboutScreen`), so both apps show the same screens. The Mac shows them in the Tomodachi window with a sidebar, in place of the System Settings–style window. The iPhone shows tabs: Play (the play screen, unchanged), Tomo, Words and Settings, with About as the last row of Settings, as in iOS's own Settings. What only one device has comes in through a slot in Settings: open at login on the Mac, reminders on the iPhone ([#88](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/88)). The credits are on About on both, since the CC BY word data needs attribution wherever the app ships. **Why:** the owner wants the two apps in line. A tab bar mirrors the Mac's sidebar and wraps the play screen without changing it. The #83 reference app has no tab bar (a mascot button opens a profile), but its home is a list, while Tomo's play screen is the app. The cost: the tab bar takes about 50 pt from the play screen, which has to fit what's left on small iPhones ([#92](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/92)).

### 2026-10-07: The MVP is Japanese only and offline
The AI page and the "I'm learning" picker are hidden on both apps. Their code stays, and they show with the testing tools (`TomoFeatures`), the AI page in Debug builds only: on 2026-10-08 the owner chose to keep AI out of every Release build, so the privacy policy can say nothing leaves the device but iCloud sync (`TomoAI.isAvailable`); a learner already on another language still sees the picker, so they can come back. The interface language (English or Japanese) stays a setting. **Why:** the owner's scope for the first release. AI needs a key and costs money per call, and the other packs aren't ready.

### 2026-10-07: Tomo is never younger than its level, and growth is checked by simulated learners
When a saved Tomo loads, its age is at least its level's age in the pack (saved and logged as a birthday), so each birthday lands on the pack's age boundary even after a pack's ages move. The self-test walks every level of every pack and simulates a perfect and a realistic learner, on a clock that only the check moves, from the first word to the first talking question ([verification.md](verification.md)). **Why:** the owner's "stays at full exp" (#89) was caught by hand; checking growth end to end found English Lv 25 had lost its ten words to a copy of Lv 26 (a `build-levels.py` bug, now fixed), the iPhone cut each evolution short, and a Mac never evolved for a birthday reached on the iPhone.

### 2026-10-07: Esc closes Tomo only when Tomo has the keyboard
Esc is a local key monitor on the island: it works once the learner has clicked Tomo or opened it from the menu (the island takes the keyboard then, and gives it back when it closes), with no permission. During a visit Tomo drops in on its own, Esc stays with the learner's app; × closes the visit, or ignoring it does. **Why:** the old global monitor needed Accessibility, which Tomodachi never asks for, so Esc never worked. A Carbon hot key would have worked anywhere, but it takes Esc from every other app while Tomo is open (vim, dialogs, full-screen video), and Tomo opens by itself while people work.

### 2026-10-07: Testing tools are hidden, and never touch the saved Tomo
"Skip to talking" and Settings → Testing show after ⌥-clicking the menu bar icon, or with the `tomoTestingTools` default ([verification.md](verification.md)); "Skip ahead a day" moves a copy of Tomo in memory, never the saved one. The microphone permission is gone while talking questions are choices. **Why:** a learner shouldn't find tools that change Tomo's clock: skipping ahead saved answers dated in the future and synced them to iCloud. The owner tests with Developer ID builds, so it isn't `#if DEBUG`.

### 2026-10-07: Coucou's agent features are deleted, not kept switched off
About 9,400 lines are gone ([coucou-fork.md](coucou-fork.md)); git history keeps them. **Why:** "switched off" wasn't off: a file dropped on the notch was copied and could be emailed, and every launch read 12 of Coucou's API keys from the Keychain. The project rename waits until no branches are open.

### 2026-10-07: When nothing counts, Tomo rests; the bar's goal fills only when the level is done
Replaces the practice offer of 2026-10-06. When nothing counts, Tomo rests (`TomoPhase.resting`), says when it's back and what's left, and stays resting when opened again; Practice is a quiet button, and **Later** is gone. The bar counts the level's best 9 words; its last tenth (`goalShare`) fills only when the level is done, and a slipped word's part turns pale. **Why:** on the iPhone, Later offered practice again every tick; the owner's bar read 97% one word short of Lv 2, with nothing saying what was left. A goal segment keeps the bar proportional, where a 97% cap still looks full.

### 2026-10-07: The first run copies #83's reference app; the data folder decides who sees it
New learners get a first run before Tomo's first visit, copied from the reference's screens that fit Tomo (its sign-in, goal, placement and level screens set nothing here; its paywall gets a reserved spot). It shows when the data folder has no saved Tomo, and is marked done in `onboarding.json` there, not UserDefaults (Debug builds share the owner's, #54); a device joining the iCloud Tomo gets "welcome back". The Mac's cadence is its visit choices, not notifications. **Why:** App Review rejected 1.0 partly for dropping learners into a round; the owner asked to copy the reference.

### 2026-10-07: Every Tomo is a blob of its own, and evolves at each birthday
Tomo is no longer a chick. Each learner's Tomo is a blob drawn from a seed (`TomoLook`), using blobatar's method and shape primitives (MIT, ported into our code, not its package; [research](research/blobatar.md)): a colour, eye style, mouth, markings and head topper for life, and at every birthday a new silhouette and proportions, a little more size and sometimes an extra, rolled from the seed and the age, with rare shapes as finds. A first version varied only colour and shape, and the owner found the crowd too alike. How different Tomos are is one setting, `TomoLook.variety` (1 = the shipped mix), picked by the owner from `TOMO_RENDER_VARIETY` sheets. Evolution is a first version: shapes re-roll at random each birthday, which doesn't yet read as growing up ([#87](https://github.com/serpcompany/tomodachi-app/issues/87)). The seed is the language pair and the second the Tomo first appeared, which was already saved and synced, so the Mac and iPhone agree with no new iCloud field, and starting over hatches a new Tomo. Widgets, Live Activities and the icons show one fixed mascot (yellow, round), because their frames are fonts made ahead of time. Faces are our own (readable at notch size), drawn in the blob's eye colour. **Why:** the owner found the chick too close to Duolingo, and a Tomo nobody else has, which changes as it grows, is a reason to come back. Accepted: the blob style is blobatar's, so other apps' avatars will look like Tomo's family; we can move our copy away from it over time.
